# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and access
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for VPN, email and the production network. Never share credentials.

## Devices
Company laptops use full-disk encryption. Personal USB drives must not be connected to factory-floor PCs. Lost devices must be reported to IT within 2 hours.

## Incident reporting
Report suspected phishing or data leaks to security@example.com immediately. Do not delete the suspicious message.
