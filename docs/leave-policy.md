# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is requested through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by March 31.

## Sick leave
Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Notify your manager before the start of your shift.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Leave may be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
