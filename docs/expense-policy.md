# Expense Policy

## Travel
Economy class applies to all flights under 6 hours. Hotels are reimbursed up to 15,000 JPY per night in Japan and 180 USD per night elsewhere. Book through the approved travel portal.

## Meals
Client meals are reimbursed up to 8,000 JPY per person with prior manager approval. Individual meals during travel are capped at 3,500 JPY per day.

## Submitting claims
Submit receipts within 30 days of the expense. Claims over 50,000 JPY need director approval. Claims without receipts are rejected.
